Amics Automations

Amics Automations is an industrial automation and engineering solutions company specialising in the design, development, manufacturing and integration of customised automation systems for production and assembly industries.

We provide turnkey solutions covering mechanical design, electrical engineering, control panel manufacturing, PLC and HMI programming, machine software development, system integration, installation, commissioning and after-sales support.

Our primary focus is to develop reliable, practical and production-oriented systems that improve manufacturing productivity, process consistency, traceability, product quality and operator safety.

⚙   OUR OPERATING PRINCIPLE

An automation system must do more than pass a demonstration. It must perform reliably under actual production conditions, remain maintainable and deliver measurable value throughout its operating life.
